A typical flight loop (500 Hz):
1. Interrupt from IMU: new data ready.
2. Read gyro and accelerometer over SPI (microseconds).
3. Convert raw counts to rad/s and g; subtract the gyro bias measured on the pad.
4. Update the attitude estimate (sensor fusion; integrating the gyro dominates during powered flight,
since the accelerometer can’t see tilt under thrust).
5. Run pitch and yaw PID loops (D term from the filtered gyro, per TVC Insight #3).
6. Clamp to gimbal limits, convert gimbal angle → servo angle → pulse width.
7. Write servo PWM (the servo itself may only act at 50–333 Hz).
8. Every Nth loop, buffer a log line; write to flash or SD in chunks
